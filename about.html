Our story

Getting a TIN number, registering a company, or applying for a passport in Tanzania often means navigating several offices, unclear requirements, and long queues. LUO SOLUTION HUB exists to close that gap — we do the running around, the form-filling, and the follow-up, and keep you updated the whole way through.

What started as help for a handful of friends and family has grown into a service covering tax registration, business licensing, land records, national ID, student loans, travel documents, and more — all handled by people who deal with these offices every single day.
